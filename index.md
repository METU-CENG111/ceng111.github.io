# CENG 111 — Introduction to Computer Engineering Concepts

{: .lede }
This is the official site of CENG 111. CENG 111 is an introductory course to Computer Engineering and Science concepts, exclusive to the undergraduates of the Department of Computer Engineering, METU.

This site is one of the means of communication between the instructors and students. The others are lectures and the course page on [odtuclass.metu.edu.tr](https://odtuclass.metu.edu.tr).

This site also contains various Python resources, as Python is used as a tool to introduce fundamental computer science concepts.

<div class="notice" markdown="1">
**Check ODTUClass daily.** The news forum on [ODTUClass](https://odtuclass.metu.edu.tr) should be checked on a daily basis for announcements. **Grades** are announced through the ODTUClass system.
</div>
